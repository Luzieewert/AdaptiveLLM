Study Instructions

In this study, you will chat with an AI assistant in two separate rounds. Each round includes two short chats about different everyday topics.

The study consists of two rounds. Depending on the assigned study condition, you will either begin with a standard AI assistant or with a personalized AI assistant. Before each round, you will receive all necessary instructions.

You can chat as you normally would with an AI assistant. There are no specific tasks or correct answers. Please write at least 3 and at most 10 messages in each chat.

After each round, you will answer a short questionnaire about your experience in the two chats. At the beginning, you will answer a few questions about your prior experience with AI systems. At the end, you will answer a few demographic questions.

There are no right or wrong answers. We are interested in your personal experience.
